# Parakh brand

## What Parakh is (approved description)
Pakistan's property market runs on incomplete information. Many housing schemes operate without full
regulatory approval, sell phases that were never sanctioned, or remain legally compliant yet fail to deliver
possession for decades. The records that would expose these risks already exist across development authority
registers, regulatory filings, court proceedings, news coverage and buyer communities, but they're fragmented,
inconsistent and practically inaccessible to the average buyer. Parakh helps buyers judge a project's
credibility before they make one of the most important financial decisions of their lives. It brings these
scattered sources together into a single, evidence-based assessment with a credibility score backed by cited
sources, so the decision rests on verifiable facts rather than on the developer's marketing.

Positioning line: **An approval check tells you if a project is legal. Parakh tells you if it is credible.**
("Approved doesn't mean safe.")

## Name
- **Parakh** (Latin) + **پرکھ** (Urdu, Nastaliq) always appear together in the logo lockup: `Parakh  پرکھ`.
- Meaning line: *Parakh · /pə·rəkh/ · to examine, to test the worth of* — Urdu: *پرکھنا · کسی چیز کی اصل قدر جانچنا*.
- Logo mark = **"Mehrab lens"**: ivory Mughal arch on a cobalt tile with an amber dot inside (files in `assets/logo/`, rules in its README).
- Wordmark (Plus Jakarta Sans 800, letter-spacing −0.03 to −0.06em) + پرکھ (Noto Nastaliq Urdu 400–700, ~0.65× the Latin size, baseline-aligned).

## Typography
- Latin: **Plus Jakarta Sans** 400–800. Headlines 800, letter-spacing −0.03 to −0.05em, line-height 1.02–1.1.
  Kickers: 700, uppercase, letter-spacing .2em, 11–24px.
- Urdu: **Noto Nastaliq Urdu** 400/600/700. Line-height ≥ 185% (Nastaliq needs room — 1.6–2.1). Size ~0.9× the
  Latin equivalent. Always `direction: rtl`, right-aligned; mirror horizontal layouts.
- Never put Latin-only glyphs (✓ ✕ ↗, digits in brand numbers) in Nastaliq — set them in Plus Jakarta Sans.
- Google Fonts: `family=Plus+Jakarta+Sans:wght@400;500;600;700;800&family=Noto+Nastaliq+Urdu:wght@400;600;700`.

## Imagery
- Pakistani architecture at dusk/warm light: Badshahi Mosque (Lahore), Shah Rukn-e-Alam (Multan), Faisal Mosque
  (Islamabad), street markets. Photos in `assets/img/` (Wikimedia Commons, credit "Photos: Wikimedia Commons
  contributors" — replace with owned photos before commercial use).
- Arched (Mughal arch) crops for city cards: top-left/right radius = half the width.
- Real footage of streets/people works best for story videos. Check every frame for real brands, society
  names, billboards and non-Pakistani landmarks.
- Use images at ≥ native resolution: never upscale (a 1280×243 panorama looked blurry as a full-frame end card).

## Voice & wording
- Plain, calm, specific. Short sentences. Buyer language over legal language ("still waiting for possession",
  not "undelivered"; "the ad said 'LDA approved'", not "unsanctioned").
- Four results, exact wording: **No serious problems found online · Some problems found – check carefully ·
  Serious problems found · Not enough information online**.
- Urdu equivalents: **کوئی سنگین مسئلہ نہیں ملا · کچھ مسائل ملے – احتیاط سے جانچیں · سنگین مسائل ملے · انٹرنیٹ پر کافی معلومات نہیں**.
- Allegations are labelled "Allegation" (الزام) unless a court has ruled.
- Taglines: "Check before you invest." · "Know a project's credibility before you invest." ·
  "جلد آ رہا ہے" (coming soon) · "خریدنے سے پہلے پرکھیں۔"

## Legal & trust rules (Pakistan: Defamation Ordinance 2002, PECA)
- Made-up names in all marketing and design examples. Real names only inside real reports, with sources.
- Every finding has a source + date; "what we did not check" is always shown.
- Right of reply and a visible "Request a correction" on every report.
- No public ranking of named projects with Parakh scores. Public pages may repeat official status only (with
  link + date).
- Parakh never takes developer money; no one can pay to change a result.
- Don't claim capabilities the product doesn't have yet (e.g. "5-agent system that never gets it wrong").
  Current truth: one working AI pipeline; four more agents planned.
