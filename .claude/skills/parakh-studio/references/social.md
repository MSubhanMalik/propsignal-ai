# Parakh on LinkedIn and social

Goal: build the founders' LinkedIn profiles through a product people care about. Stories beat explanations.

## Where to post
- Upload **natively on personal profiles** (company pages and reposts get little reach). Tag the company page
  and partners; links go in the first comment.
- Stagger partners: one person per day, each with a **different cut** of the same film (full teaser / problem
  cut ~18s / reveal cut ~15s) and their own angle (problem · build · design & Urdu).
- Company page re-uploads the week's best post 2–3 days later.

## Weekly rhythm (3 partners)
~8 posts/week total, 2–3 per person: 2–3 videos, 3 text posts, 2–3 image/carousel posts, 1–2 company-page
posts. Post 9–11am Pakistan time on weekdays. Partners comment meaningfully within 30 minutes; reply to every
comment in the first hour; spend 15 min/day commenting on others' posts. Keep it up for 6–8 weeks.

| Day | Post |
|---|---|
| Mon | Video: teaser + personal story |
| Tue | Text: "Approved doesn't mean safe." |
| Wed | Carousel: "5 questions to ask before you pay for a plot" (NOC, approved layout plan, which phase, possession date in writing, who owns the land) |
| Thu | Video: problem cut + why we're building it |
| Fri | Image: Urdu screens / report design — "Designing for people who don't read English" |
| Sat | Video: reveal cut |
| Sun | Company page re-upload |

## Formats
LinkedIn feed 1080×1350 (4:5) with burned-in English subtitles; 20–45s; first 2 seconds = a person, real
footage or a strong line. Reels/Status 1080×1920.

## Post template
> [One-line true personal hook — e.g. "My uncle booked a plot in 2012. He's still waiting."]
>
> The records that could have warned him were public all along — on authority websites, court lists and old
> news. Just never in one place.
>
> So we're building Parakh (پرکھ): one place to check a property project's credibility before you invest, in
> Urdu and English.
>
> Coming soon. What would you want it to check first?
>
> #Pakistan #RealEstate #AI #BuildInPublic

Only use true stories ("Most families I know have a plot story" is a safe alternative). Never name a real
society/developer negatively. Claim only what exists (one working AI pipeline; four more agents planned).

## Competitor note
Approval lookups (which.pk's society approval check, LDA's own status search) answer "is it approved?".
Parakh answers "is it credible?": phase-level approval, ads vs records, delivery history, court/news/complaints,
developer's other projects, one sourced score, Urdu, PDF with QR, change alerts.
